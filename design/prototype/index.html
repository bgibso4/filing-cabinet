Try the flows
  • 1 · Scan and fileTap Scan. The first time, iOS asks for the camera; Don’t Allow shows the “camera off” sheet on the next tap (reset with Camera access above). Pick something to pretend-scan. With Apple Intelligence on you get the slim card: Save, or tap it or drag it up for the full sheet; with it off you get the sheet with nothing picked. Try the 3-page letter, or the Rescan arrow top right. Saving flies the page into its folder on Home, or into its tile inside a collection. Tick “Make the next save fail” to see Couldn’t save.
  • 2 · Find a scanOpen a favorite from Home, browse Wallet or Receipts, or search “renew”, “heat” or “laptop”. Try the filter chips.
  • 3 · View and changeOpen the Corner Market receipt with 5 days left. Tap the title or Info, rename it, change its collection or expiry, heart it, swipe a 3-page lease.
  • 4 · Cleanup and TrashMove a scan to Trash and watch it leave. Open Trash from Home: tap a scan to restore or delete it, long-press for a menu, or Select several. Empty Trash asks first.
  • 5 · SettingsGear on Home. Flip the two switches, or open Collections and change how long Receipts keeps scans: try 1 month to see the count and confirm.

Favorites: long-press a tile to drag it when the sort is Custom order. Elsewhere, long-press a tile to favorite it.
