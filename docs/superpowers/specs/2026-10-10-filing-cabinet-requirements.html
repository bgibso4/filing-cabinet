Filing Cabinet · v1 requirements

Keeps what matters. Shreds the rest.

A private, Photos-style library just for personal documents. It files each scan itself and cleans up after itself. This document defines what v1 does. The design spec defines how.

01 · Intent

Audience & intent

v1 is built for the author's own use, with an App Store release as the intended next step. It must work well for its author, and anything that would block strangers from using it is listed under Before App Store release.

Scan my driver's license or insurance card and find it instantly later.

Snap receipts that quietly disappear after they stop mattering.

Photograph class notes and forms without polluting my camera roll.

Grab a throwaway doc (a coat-check tag) that deletes itself in a week.

These four jobs were confirmed on 2026-10-10 as the complete set. No others are in scope.

Updated 2026-10-25 after the screens were designed, and 2026-10-29 with the app icon. Lines that changed are corrected in place, and everything decided during design is listed in 06 · Design changes. The decisions board on the Mocks canvas stays as the record of 2026-10-10.

Where this document and the design spec (2026-07-12-filing-cabinet-design.md) conflict, this document wins.

02 · Core model

Collections, expiry, cleanup

Every scan belongs to exactly one collection. A collection is what the document is, and it supplies the scan's default lifespan.

Wallet

IDs and cards: license, insurance, membership, business cards

Forever

Receipts

Purchase receipts

3 months

Notes

Handwritten and class notes

Forever

Documents

Forms, letters, anything else

Forever

Throwaways

Short-lived scraps: coat-check tag, parking stub

1 week

Recent and All are views over every scan, not collections.

Automatic filing

  • The app checks whether Apple Intelligence is available using the on-device model's availability API. If it is, the model suggests a collection and the review sheet comes pre-filled with it.
  • If it isn't available (the device isn't eligible, Apple Intelligence is turned off, or the model isn't downloaded yet), you pick the collection on the review sheet. Every other feature works the same.
  • A wrong guess always costs one tap to fix.
  • Accuracy is checked with a throwaway experiment on 20–30 of the author's real scans before the implementation plan. If it's poor, v1 ships with manual picking only, and the review sheet doesn't change.

Expiry

  • A scan's expiry is its collection's default lifespan unless you've set a custom expiry for it.
  • Expiry is counted from when the scan was taken. Time left is shown in years when a year or more remains, in whole months from 60 days, and in days under 60 days; the last week is orange.
  • Forever means the scan is never cleaned up. This replaces the design spec's separate "cleanup exemption" toggle.
  • Changing a scan's collection moves its expiry to the new collection's default. A custom expiry is kept.
  • The new expiry is still counted from the scan date, never from the day of the move. If that date has already passed, the app asks first ("Move to Throwaways?" · Cancel / Move); Move sends the scan to Trash, where it can be restored for 30 days.
  • Changing a collection's default lifespan in Settings applies to every scan still on that default, existing scans included. Scans with a custom expiry aren't affected.

Cleanup

In libraryShows its expiry status
→
Expiry passesIn Trash from that moment, even if the app isn't open. You saw the expiry when you saved
→
Trash30 days to restore
→
DeletedPermanently. Files are removed the next time the app runs

There is no archive in v1. Being in Trash is worked out from dates, not done by a background job: a scan past its expiry is in Trash, and a Trash item past its 30 days is gone from view, whether or not the app has run. iOS background time is unpredictable, so nothing the person sees depends on it. Removing the files themselves happens when the app next opens or gets background time.

A scan already in Trash stays there when a lifespan or its collection changes; a scan a change sends to Trash gets the full 30 days from that day.

03 · User stories

User stories

What the mocks must cover. The home screen layout is decided in the mocks.

Capture & filing
1

Scan

One tap from home opens the document camera. A scan can have multiple pages. Cancelling saves nothing.

2

Review & save

After capture, a review sheet shows these, and then you save:

  • a title taken from the scan's text, which you can edit;
  • the collection, pre-filled when automatic filing is available and a required pick otherwise (a slim card with a suggestion, the full sheet without one);
  • the expiry, defaulting to the collection's lifespan. Quick choices are 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 1 year and Forever, plus a custom date.
  • A Rescan button reopens the camera; cancelling it keeps the pages you had.
3

Out of the camera roll

Scans are never written to Photos unless you explicitly export them.

Finding
4

Browse

Each collection (Wallet, Receipts, Notes, Documents, Throwaways), plus Recent and All, is one tap from home.

5

Search

Full-text search across the recognized text of every scan. Results show thumbnails.

6

Favorites

You can favorite any scan, and favorites are one tap from home, so you can pull up your license at a counter.

Viewing & editing
7

Detail view

  • Swipe between pages and pinch to zoom at full resolution.
  • See the collection and the expiry status ("23 days left" / "Kept forever").
8

Edit anytime

The title, collection, expiry and favorite can be changed whenever you like, following the expiry rules.

9

Export

Share as PDF or images, or save to Photos or Files.

10

Delete

Move to Trash asks first, then moves the scan with no note or Undo afterwards.

Self-cleaning
11

Auto-trash

A scan moves to Trash the moment its expiry passes, even when the app isn't open. Notifications are scheduled in advance, at most one a day in the morning, worded as a favour, for example "Cleaned up 3 receipts" (up to 3 scans are named; 4 or more show only the count), and rescheduled whenever a scan, its expiry or a lifespan changes. iOS caps scheduled notifications at 64, so they cover the next 64 days with expiries and are topped up each time the app opens. With notifications off, Home shows a one-time banner after scans move and the Trash row shows its count.

12

Trash

  • See what's in it, restore a scan, delete one now, or empty it.
  • Items are permanently deleted 30 days after entering Trash. For an expired scan that is 30 days after its expiry. Delete now and Empty Trash take effect immediately.
  • A restored scan is set to Forever, so it doesn't immediately expire again.
  • Favorites keep their heart in Trash and come back as favorites; they expire like any other scan.
13

Lifespan settings

In Settings › Collections you can edit each collection's default lifespan ("Keep scans for"). Scans still on the default follow the change.

Privacy
14

Documents stay on device

Scans stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud backup. There's no account, and automatic filing runs on-device. Your documents never leave your phone: the only thing sent is anonymous usage and failure counts, with no document content (no images, text, titles, extracted values or custom collection names).

04 · Non-functional

Non-functional requirements

The parts you don't see: where things live, how fast they are, and what can never go wrong.

Platform

  • iPhone only (iPad maybe later), portrait only, English only, SwiftUI.
  • iOS 27 minimum. Apple Intelligence doesn't require it, since Foundation Models arrived in iOS 26. iOS 27 was chosen so there are no availability branches, the newest APIs are available, and most active iPhones will be on 27 by an App Store release.

Backup

The library is covered by the normal iCloud device backup. There's no iCloud sync in v1, but the data model is kept sync-ready.

Performance

  • A library of ~2,000 scans scrolls smoothly.
  • Grid cells only ever decode thumbnails.
  • Memory use doesn't grow with library size.

No data loss

  • Saving is all-or-nothing: page files are written before the database record, and orphaned files are cleaned up.
  • The only permanent deletions are the 30-day Trash purge and the user deleting from Trash.

Graceful fallback

Without Apple Intelligence, every feature works, and the collection is picked by hand.

Appearance

Light and dark mode both ship in v1 and follow the system setting. Every screen is designed for both.

Accessibility

Supports Dynamic Type up to the largest sizes, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion, and meets WCAG AA contrast. Details in 06.

Storage

Full-quality page images are HEIC files in the app's private folder. The database stores information about each scan and the file names of its pages, never image data. Thumbnails (~400 px) are generated once when a scan is saved and kept in Caches, where they can be regenerated if iOS clears them. Nothing is visible in the Files app.

06 · Design changes

Decided while designing the screens

Settled 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-29 in Claude Design. Every chosen screen, the prototype, the screen sources, the design tokens and the app icon are in design/ in this repo (start with design/README.md).

First launch

  • Launch screen is plain: Home's background colour (#f2f2f7 light, #000000 dark) with the status bar. No logo, no words. Set through the launch screen colour setting.
  • Two screens: a welcome with four plain points (scan in one tap, sorted into collections, cleans up after itself, private by design), then "Know when it cleans up", followed by iOS's notification prompt.
  • The camera is asked on the first Scan, with our line: "Filing Cabinet uses the camera to scan documents. Scans stay on this iPhone." If denied, every Scan shows "Camera access is off" with Open Settings.
  • The Scan button always reads "Scan", including on an empty Home.

Home

  • Collection rows are 60pt, each with a round badge in the collection's colour and its own symbol (Wallet a card, Receipts a receipt, Notes a pencil, Documents a page, Throwaways a ticket).
  • After a cleanup, "4 scans moved to Trash · View" sits under the title.

Scanning and review

  • With an Apple Intelligence suggestion: a slim card (title, collection and expiry, Save) that opens into the full sheet. Without one: the full sheet with "Choose a collection".
  • Choosing a collection fills in its expiry, which can still be changed.
  • A page pill shows "1 page" or "Page 2 of 3"; multi-page scans swipe and show a page strip.
  • Rescan (circular-arrow button, top right) reopens the camera; finishing replaces the pages, cancelling keeps them.
  • While Apple Intelligence reads the scan (about 2 s in testing on a Mac; to be measured on iPhone): the slim card appears as soon as the camera closes, with the scan showing. Its title and collection rows shimmer above "Reading your scan…", Save stays off, and a soft scanner bar sweeps down the page. When the suggestion lands, the rows fade in, Save turns on, a light haptic taps and VoiceOver reads the suggestion. You can tap the card and choose a collection before then; your choice wins. With Reduce Motion: no shimmer or sweep, plain rows, then a quick fade. Mock: https://claude.ai/artifact/SZ5Ntg5vThvnYN3KAgv14Q
  • If no suggestion arrives within about 5 seconds, it stops waiting and the card grows into the full sheet (the one designed for phones without Apple Intelligence), with the scan shrinking to make room. The title comes from the text without Apple Intelligence, so it is less trustworthy and the sheet keeps it right there to edit; the collection is required before Save. Mock: https://claude.ai/artifact/ULgeFhB5rS6qNzMwLebT5N
  • The review closes with an X. Cancel asks "Discard this scan?".
  • A failed save (usually a full iPhone) shows an alert with Not now and Try again; nothing is lost.
  • After saving there is no note. Save and Discard slide review down like a closing sheet, back to where you were (the camera's Cancel slides it down too); then the collection's count ticks up with a small pop. With Reduce Motion it is a quick fade and the count ticks without a pop.

Viewing and changing a scan

  • Viewing works like Photos. Details open as a sheet where every row is editable straight away (Undo for a collection move); there is no Edit button.
  • Expiry is always counted from the scan date. Moving a scan into a collection whose time has already passed asks first; Move sends it to Trash.
  • Move to Trash asks first. No "Moved to Trash" note and no Undo afterwards.

Trash

  • A grid like a collection. Each tile shows its days left (orange in the last week). No reason is shown or stored.
  • Tap opens the scan with Delete now and Restore. Long-press gives Open, Restore and Delete now. Select picks several; Cancel leaves select mode.
  • Delete now, deleting several, and Empty Trash all confirm. Restoring never does.
  • A restored scan is kept forever. Favorites keep their heart, come back as favorites, and expire like any other scan.

Collections and settings

  • Receipts are kept 3 months, shown that way everywhere.
  • Settings: Collections, an Apple Intelligence switch ("Suggests titles and collections"), a Notifications switch ("A note when scans move to Trash"), Send feedback, and the footer "Filing Cabinet 1.0 · Private by design".
  • Collections opens a list; each collection has "Keep scans for" (1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 1 year, Forever) with the shortening confirmation and count.

Notifications

  • Sent after cleanup, worded as a favour: "Cleaned up 3 receipts".
  • Up to 3 scans are named; 4 or more show only the count. Mixed collections are called "scans".
  • Every collection counts, Throwaways included. At most one a day, in the morning.

Search

  • One ranked list with recently viewed scans, recent searches and collection filters; title matches first, the match in bold.
  • First use (nothing viewed or searched yet) shows only the filters. A search is saved when you press Search or open a result.

Time left

  • A collection's lifespan uses its friendly unit: "Kept 3 months", "Kept 1 week", "Kept forever".
  • A scan's countdown: a year or more shows years ("1 year left"); 60 days to a year shows whole months, rounded down ("2 months left"); under 60 days shows days ("45 days left"); the last week is orange. Trash always shows days.
  • When a scan's whole lifespan is a week or less, only its last day is orange.

Extras in v1

Drawn by iOS; ours are the words, the Scan symbol and what happens next.

  • Scan control for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button. It unlocks if needed and opens straight into the camera; saving or cancelling lands on Home. Before first launch has run it shows the two welcome screens first; with camera access off it shows the usual sheet; with a review already open it brings that back.
  • Shortcuts and Siri: "Scan a document" (same flow as the control) and "Find a scan", which takes text and opens Search with those results.
  • Spotlight: scan titles appear in iPhone search; tapping one opens that scan. Titles only.

Scope and business

  • iPhone only (iPad maybe later), portrait only, English only for v1.
  • Fully free: no purchases, no paywall, no ads.
  • Scans are included in iCloud Backup. Privacy wording: "Private by design · No account. Scans stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud backup."

App icon

  • Scan corners around a folder, in the app's own colours: ink corners (#1c1c1e) on the app background (#f2f2f7) in light, reversed in dark; the folder is terracotta (#c0533a) in both. iOS makes the tinted and clear versions.
  • Layers for Icon Composer and previews are in design/app-icon/.

Accessibility

  • Contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 text, 3:1 symbols). Light "soon" orange is #ad500e; light destructive text uses #d70015 (dark keeps #ff453a); collection badges use the deeper colours in both themes (wallet #3f6fa3, receipts #c0533a, notes #7a5aa8, documents #4b8160, throwaways #b98a1f).
  • Large text: everything follows Dynamic Type up to the largest sizes and wraps rather than cutting off. Collection grids drop to two columns (Wallet to one); the review card stacks with a full-width Save; detail rows put the label above the value; Settings descriptions wrap under the name.
  • VoiceOver: a tile reads as one item ("Corner Market, Receipts, 2 days left, favorite") with actions for Open, Favorite and Move to Trash (Restore and Delete now in Trash). Icon-only buttons are named. The page pill reads "Page 2 of 3". Save, Restore, Delete and moves announce the result. Colour is never the only signal.
  • Reduce Motion: zooms, the card-to-sheet growth and review or the camera sliding away become quick cross-fades; the count on Home ticks without a pop.

Haptics

  • Light tap: favoriting, choosing from a menu, a switch. Success: a scan saved or restored. Warning, just before the confirmation: Move to Trash, Delete now, Empty Trash. The standard lift on long-press. Nothing for plain navigation.

Rating prompt

  • Apple's standard prompt, asked only right after a successful save, once someone has saved 10 scans on at least 3 different days. Never during a review, a confirmation or onboarding.

Build for what's coming

  • Collections are data (name, colour, symbol, lifespan), not a fixed list of five, so custom collections only need new screens.
  • One review screen, many ways in: camera, Rescan, the Scan control, Shortcuts, and later the share extension.
  • Words in one place: every user-facing string in a string catalogue from day one.
  • Layouts that stretch: adaptive layouts and design tokens, so iPad needs no redesign.
  • App Intents as the backbone for the Scan control, Shortcuts, Siri and Spotlight, so widgets and AI search plug in later.
  • Apple Intelligence behind one switch: suggestions today, search by meaning later.

Later (after v1)

  • Apple Intelligence search · custom collections · share extension, then widgets · locked scans with Face ID · export all scans.
07 · Future

Future (not v1)

Each item becomes a GitHub issue once the repo has a remote.

Before App Store release

  • Hand-written classification rules for devices without Apple Intelligence. Examples: Wallet from card shape (ID-1 ~1.59:1, business cards ~1.75:1) plus keywords; Receipts from money amounts plus "TOTAL"/"TAX" plus a date.
  • Document lock for sensitive documents (design below).

Someday

  • Field search: find a field such as "policy number" across all scans and copy its value.
  • Tags.
  • Custom collections (add, delete, rename, colour, icon). Build v1 so this needs new screens, not a new model.
  • Apple Intelligence search (matching by meaning), behind the same switch as suggestions.
  • A share extension ("Save to Filing Cabinet" for PDFs and photos) next, then widgets.
  • An Expiring view: everything with an expiry, soonest first.
  • iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Importing and classifying the existing camera-roll backlog.
  • Showing scans in the Files app.
  • More cleanup rule types (count-based, size-based).
  • Splitting a multi-page capture into separate scans on the review sheet.

Intended design: document lock

Locking is per scan, not a collection. A locked passport is still a Wallet item kept forever, so a "Sensitive" collection would clash with one collection per scan.

  • Any scan can be marked Locked, from the review sheet or the detail view. It stays in its collection, with a blurred thumbnail and an optionally hidden title.
  • Opening a locked scan requires device owner authentication: Face ID or Touch ID, with an automatic fallback to the device passcode.
  • A locked scan's text is left out of search results until you unlock, so a search can't leak a preview.
  • Each collection has a "Lock new scans automatically" setting. It's on by default for Wallet.
  • An optional, separate whole-app lock covers everything.
  • No device passcode: locking is unavailable, the toggle is disabled, and a note says to set a passcode. If the passcode is removed later, locked scans become viewable again, with a banner saying locking is paused until a passcode is set.
  • No app PIN. It would mean owning PIN recovery and give a false sense of security on a phone with no passcode.

v1 needs no work for this. A per-scan isLocked flag and a per-collection setting can be added later.
