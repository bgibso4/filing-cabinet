Question 1 · How the bar is laid out

Three ways to put Search down there, beside today's Home for comparison. Use the switches above to see each on a collection (which has no search today), in dark, and at the largest text size.

I'd pick A. It is the Passwords shape you pointed at, the field is wide enough to read as a search bar, and Scan is still the only dark, filled thing on the screen. B keeps Scan exactly where your thumb knows it but Search stops being a bar. C gives both full size and costs a second row of the screen on every page.

Question 2 · What tapping Search does

This one decides how big the job is. Today the Search screen slides up with its own field at the top. If the bar is at the bottom, the field can stay there while you type, as Passwords does.

I'd pick 2. With 1 your thumb taps at the bottom and the field jumps to the top, which undoes most of the point. 2 is more work: the Search screen's field, close button and filter chips all change place, and its design in design/ gets redrawn.
