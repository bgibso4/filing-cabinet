Privacy

Filing Cabinet · Last updated November 13, 2026

Filing Cabinet keeps your documents on your iPhone. It does not collect anything about you or your scans, and nothing is sent anywhere.

No account, no collection

There is no sign-up and no account. The app has no tracking, no advertising and no third-party analytics. It does not connect to any server of ours or anyone else's.

The app counts what happens in it (for example, that a scan was saved) only in your iPhone's own system log, never with your scan's contents, and that log does not leave your phone.

Your scans

Scans, and the text and titles the app reads from them, are stored on your iPhone in the app's own storage. Like other app data, they may be included in your iPhone's backups (such as iCloud Backup) if you have those turned on.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan documents.

Photos

The app asks for add-only access to Photos, and only when you choose to save a scan there. It cannot see your photo library.

Notifications

Reminders about scans that are about to be cleaned up are scheduled on your iPhone. They do not come from a server.

Reading and suggestions

Reading text on a scan, and the titles and collections suggested with Apple Intelligence, happen on your iPhone. Your scans are not sent anywhere to do this.

iPhone search

So you can find a scan from Spotlight, the app adds each scan's title, its collection and page count, and a small preview image to your iPhone's search index. The index is stored on your iPhone and is protected whenever the phone is locked. The text read from your scans is not added.

Feedback

Send feedback opens a draft in your Mail app. It contains what you write plus the app version, the iOS version and your iPhone model. You can read and change it before you send it, and nothing is sent unless you do.
