Support

Filing Cabinet for iPhone

Questions, problems and ideas are welcome. Email filingcabinet.app@gmail.com and Benjamin Gibson, who makes the app, will reply.

Sending feedback from the app

Settings has a Send feedback row. It opens a draft in your mail app with the app version and your iPhone model already filled in, which helps with a problem report. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

A scan is missing

Each collection keeps its scans for a set time. When that time is up, a scan moves to Trash and waits there for 30 days before it is deleted. Open Trash from Home to restore it.

To keep scans for longer, open Settings, then Collections, and choose how long each collection keeps them.

Scans are not named or filed for me

Titles and collections are suggested with Apple Intelligence, on your iPhone. If Apple Intelligence is turned off in iPhone Settings, or your iPhone doesn't have it, the app makes no suggestions and you choose them yourself.

Your data

Scans stay on your iPhone. The privacy policy says what the app stores and what it sends.
